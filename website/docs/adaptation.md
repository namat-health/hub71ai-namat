# An honest pre-launch page

**Sole company source:** *NamatHealth_September2026.pdf*, 13 pages, supplied by Borja. Slide numbers below are PDF page numbers. The pages were visually reviewed because much of the text is outlined and missing from text extraction.

The deck describes an intended care model and a planned pilot. Slide 13 leaves legal launch scope and costs unsettled; slide 11 marks DoH licensing as open. The user further clarified that no membership plans or prices are final, and the medical team is one doctor co-founder. These limitations govern every design.

“The idea,” “The founders,” “The people behind Namat,” “Where we are today,” “An idea taking shape” and the navigation labels are editorial labels for these sourced sections. “Join the waitlist,” “Stay in the loop,” “Be part of what comes next,” consent, validation and status messages are functional copy authorized by the requested waitlist. They promise no priority, discount, launch date, appointment or medical outcome.

The privacy note describes the implemented data flow, not a company credential derived from the deck. It is labelled a preview pending a final approved company notice.

## Deliberate omissions

No pricing, finalized plans, biomarker counts, app screenshots, sample medical results, testimonials, patient count, clinical team beyond the named doctor co-founder, partnerships, insurance coverage, integrations, regulatory approval, promised launch date or health benefit statistics appear.

The deck’s market-size and life-expectancy figures are investor context rather than necessary visitor persuasion. The celebrity portraits on slide 3 are not Namat advisers. The medical and employer logos in the founders’ biography are not a partnership strip. The diagrams on slide 11 are proposed operations, not proof that lab, imaging or at-home delivery arrangements are signed.

## Design and asset rules

- The original Namat wordmark comes from the user’s Claude Design project and is reproduced in its original construction. It is not regenerated by Midjourney.
- Midjourney was used only for two atmospheric images: distant walkers in a sand landscape, and an empty coastal landscape. Neither is represented as a documentary photograph of Namat.
- No generated doctor portraits, branded clinics, fake dashboards or clinical measurements were made.
- One shared content source and one shared form prevent the five versions from drifting factually.
- Site previews remain protected in Vercel. The domain has not been switched to a live public service.

## What the five-way comparison can tell us

The variants are design alternatives, not a controlled A/B test. Each changes several visual variables at once. After selecting a direction, test one hypothesis at a time—for example, the hero composition or the position of the care-cycle explanation—while keeping the content and conversion event stable. No analytics provider or tracking claim has been invented or silently added.
