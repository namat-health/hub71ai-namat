## Illustrative imagery

Generated in the user's logged-in Midjourney account on 17 September 2026. The user explicitly requested Midjourney for photography. Two generations (four candidates each) were made; one candidate from each was downloaded using Midjourney's Download Image control. The PNGs were converted to WebP without retouching for delivery size. Original downloads remain in the user's Downloads folder.

- `public/assets/dune.webp`: job `674d0b7b-8f7f-4819-b098-ad435d302364`, image 0. [Source job](https://www.midjourney.com/jobs/674d0b7b-8f7f-4819-b098-ad435d302364?index=0). Prompt: editorial aerial photograph of two anonymous adults in an immense warm sand landscape, very small figures, long shadows and negative space; no medical setting, buildings, equipment, logo or text.
- `public/assets/coast.webp`: job `e8cb3dc6-af8f-4405-bd87-b9c60f3a099c`, image 2. [Source job](https://www.midjourney.com/jobs/e8cb3dc6-af8f-4405-bd87-b9c60f3a099c?index=2). Prompt: quiet empty Arabian coastal landscape, deep teal water, limestone/sand shoreline, restrained natural light and negative space; no people, buildings, boats, medical objects, logos or text.

These are atmospheric illustrations, not actual locations of Namat operations, documentary members, clinicians, treatment imagery or proof of outcomes. Used as decorative backgrounds with the content remaining in accessible HTML.
