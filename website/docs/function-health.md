# Function Health — competitive study

Accessed **17 September 2026**. Official public pages only. This is a study of Function’s marketing, not independent validation of its clinical claims. Homepage order and visual treatment were inspected in a separate browser tab; supporting offer, eligibility, trust and product pages were read directly. No account was created or form submitted. Dynamic announcements can vary: this browser displayed an HSA/FSA announcement, while the text crawler returned an imaging announcement.

## Core promise and audience

Function presents ongoing awareness of the body as a route to a longer, healthier life. Its mission is aspirational longevity, supported by recurring testing, interpretation and longitudinal tracking. **Design interpretation:** it addresses people who want agency and clarity before symptoms force action; the emphasis is broader than metabolic health alone. [About Function](https://www.functionhealth.com/about)

The offer is US-focused. The FAQ states eligibility for adults 18 or older, excluding people currently pregnant, and access through Quest locations. This is an established direct-to-consumer service with operational eligibility rules, not a pre-launch concept. Its FAQ frames the service as additional insight alongside existing care. **Design interpretation:** the addressable visitor is already willing to investigate and potentially pay for preventive information. [Function FAQs](https://www.functionhealth.com/faq)

## Landing offer

The displayed US offer is **$365 annually**, also expressed as **$1 per day**. Membership marketing includes 160+ annual lab tests, two testing rounds, reviewed results, personalized guidance and a central record. HSA/FSA eligibility and independence from insurance are prominent. Imaging is an optional addition, not included in the base price. The footer qualifies the $365 offer as first-year membership and identifies New York/New Jersey exceptions to the stated prepaid-test inclusion. [Pricing](https://www.functionhealth.com/pricing)

The FAQ clarifies that the total comprises 100+ tests initially and 60+ at a midyear follow-up, usually 3–6 months later; additional on-demand tests cost extra. This distinction matters: the annual total is not 160+ distinct biomarkers in one appointment. [Function FAQs](https://www.functionhealth.com/faq)

The test library separates included tests from add-ons, provides category navigation and search, and labels calculations derived from tests. **Design interpretation:** this gives detail-oriented visitors a way to inspect the offer instead of relying on a headline count. [What Function tests](https://www.functionhealth.com/what-we-test)

## Trust system

- **People and institutions:** named medical/scientific advisers include Mark Hyman, JoAnn Manson and Andrew Huberman, with visible roles and affiliations. The About page also displays awards and partnerships. These are Function’s representations; institutional affiliations should not automatically be read as institutional endorsements. [About](https://www.functionhealth.com/about)
- **Process:** clinician review is explained as part of the results journey, alongside health-history context, shareable results and follow-up. This supports credibility through an understandable procedure, rather than a badge alone. [How it works](https://www.functionhealth.com/how-it-works)
- **Member experience:** a dedicated testimonial library combines videos, written stories, names and verified-member labels. Themes include discovering issues, interpreting symptoms and taking action. Testimonials are marketing evidence of individual experiences, not proof of typical outcomes. [Member stories](https://www.functionhealth.com/what-people-say)
- **Data handling:** a dedicated security page states encryption, controls over access, independent reviews, SOC 2 Type II compliance and HIPAA alignment. These are precise, separate assertions—not a generic promise that everything is “secure.” [Security](https://www.functionhealth.com/security)

**Design interpretation:** credibility is cumulative. Human authority answers “who stands behind this”; process answers “what happens”; members answer “does anyone like me use it”; detailed product and security pages answer “can I examine the substance.” This architecture depends on real assets and operational maturity.

## Conversion mechanics

The primary CTA is **“Start testing”**; the membership card uses **“Join Function.”** Both lead toward signup. The header remains visible while scrolling. No homepage waitlist or countdown was observed. [Homepage](https://www.functionhealth.com/)

Price is visible at entry and developed further in a large membership card. Daily framing lowers the perceived unit cost; annual billing remains stated. The comparison to a much higher potential cost is a strong value anchor. **Interpretation:** Function makes buying feel economically accessible while keeping the package simple. Namat has no basis for reproducing that price argument. [Pricing](https://www.functionhealth.com/pricing)

Secondary conversion exists through a footer email signup offering health guides. This captures interested visitors who are not ready to purchase. [Member stories page/footer](https://www.functionhealth.com/what-people-say)

Process pages repeatedly connect explanation to the same signup destination. The zip-code lookup addresses service availability before commitment. **Interpretation:** each CTA follows an answered question; repetition is useful because the page is long, not because the button wording constantly changes. [How it works](https://www.functionhealth.com/how-it-works)

## Visual language and motion

The homepage opens with full-bleed cinematic human imagery: close portraits and outdoor movement, sliced into diagonal bands. White typography overlays the darkened imagery. The headline is left-aligned; concise offer facts sit beside it. Hero animation exposes a pause control. [Homepage, directly observed](https://www.functionhealth.com/)

The supporting process page carries the same editorial system: warm ivory surfaces, charcoal text, terracotta accents, generous whitespace, large serif headings with italic emphasis, rounded panels, hairline dividers and pill-shaped buttons. Typography inspected in the browser reports **Financier Display** for headings and **FT Base** for CTA text. Brand serif typography provides character while the sans-serif handles navigation and functional reading. Illustrations and product/data visuals make invisible processes legible. [Process page, directly observed](https://www.functionhealth.com/how-it-works)

Member stories use portraits and video rather than anonymous decorative avatars. **Interpretation:** visual humanity helps prevent the technical proposition from becoming a laboratory catalogue. [Member stories](https://www.functionhealth.com/what-people-say)

The observable design alternates immersive imagery with calmer information blocks. **Interpretation:** the contrast gives visitors relief between persuasive sections and creates a deliberate reading rhythm. Motion should support that rhythm; it is not a prerequisite for credibility or conversion.

## Transferable patterns for Namat — design recommendations, not Namat claims

1. **Keep one clear next step.** A visitor should understand the venture’s supported idea, then find the waitlist without hunting.
2. **Explain before elaborating.** A short, deck-supported model can make an early venture understandable without promising an operational journey that is not ready.
3. **Use genuine founding authority.** If the deck names and supports the doctor co-founder’s role, a modest founder introduction can provide a real human point of trust. Do not turn one doctor into a fictional clinical team.
4. **Borrow the reading rhythm.** Alternate concise statement, supported explanation, visual pause and conversion. This is transferable independently of Function’s scale.
5. **Treat warmth as a design choice.** Editorial typography and human/lifestyle imagery can feel considered without borrowing Function’s content, visual assets or implied clinical endorsements.

**Drop unless supported by Namat’s deck:** memberships, price anchors, insurance eligibility, testing quantities, disease-detection claims, measured outcomes, operational timelines, location availability, partner logos, press, awards, testimonials, board members and compliance credentials. Do not create a fake product dashboard whose numbers imply real Namat results. Do not add scarcity or a launch date to compensate for the lack of a priced offer.

For a pre-launch page, the persuasive burden is smaller and different: clarify the supported proposition and invite interest. Function’s mature service uses breadth, logistics, authority and economic proof to justify purchase; Namat should not imitate the quantity of proof by inventing it.
